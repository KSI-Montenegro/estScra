## Original listing description

Forest apartment with sea view. Perfect for those who dream of peace and quiet in nature with only bird noise and the wind in the trees. This recently constructed property is set on six acres of woodland not far from Herceg Novi and the Croatian boarder. The building has been designed with the environment in mind and generates mainly all electricity from solar and the water supply to the house is connected to the adjacent mountain spring. . There are various facilities available on the grounds for you to use which include a covered yoga deck space, rock built plunge pool, bbq area and outdoor shower. There are also various hiking routes and hiking trails leading from the property. It is advisable to have use of a car in this location however there is a bus service that runs to the village four times per day connecting you to Herceg Novi and beyond. The apartment is located in the main building the accommodation is as follows: -Entrance hallway with wardrobe storage space, open plan living space thoughtfully arranged to incorporate lounge sitting area, dining space, kitchenette with fridge, hob and various kitchen accessories, large double bed. Separate bathroom with shower and WC. Outside private decking with table and chairs. Utility costs water electricity included in the monthly rent.
